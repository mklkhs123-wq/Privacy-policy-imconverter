Privacy Policy — IMConverter

Last updated: September 13, 2026

IMConverter ("the app", "we", "us") is an image and PDF conversion tool for Android. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at shrimenu123@gmail.com.

1. Your photos and files

Your images and PDFs never leave your device.

  • All conversion, compression, resizing, cropping, and PDF creation happens locally on your phone.
  • We do not upload, transmit, or store copies of your photos or files on any server we control.
  • Converted files are saved only to your device's local storage (Pictures/ImageConverter and Download/ImageConverter folders).

2. Permissions the app requests

  • Photos/Media: to let you select images to convert.
  • Camera: to let you capture a photo directly for conversion, if you choose to.
  • Notifications: to let the app notify you when a conversion or export finishes.
  • Internet / Network state: used only to display advertisements (see Section 3) and, once, to download an open-source PDF library component during setup. It is not used to access or transmit your photos.

You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device's Settings → Apps → IMConverter → Permissions.

3. Advertising

IMConverter shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free to use. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, general device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly.

You can learn how Google collects and uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings (manage ad personalization)
  • How Google uses data from AdMob partner apps

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying ads (see Section 3).

We do not use analytics SDKs, crash-tracking SDKs, or any service that reads or transmits your images.

5. Data retention

Because we do not collect or store your personal files or account data on any server, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Any advertising data is retained according to Google's own retention policies, linked above.

6. Children's privacy

IMConverter is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown through the app follow Google's applicable policies for treating users appropriately based on the app's audience.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or change how ads are served. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how the app works, email us at shrimenu123@gmail.com.
